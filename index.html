01 / About

Competitive community Dota, kept simple.

Riftline Open is an independently operated amateur tournament. The event is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve Corporation. Our goal is to provide organized matches, clear administration and consistent lobby settings for participating teams.

Open entry

European amateur stacks may apply. Final participation is confirmed by tournament administration.

Online event

Matches are played online on European Dota 2 servers with tournament administrators available for disputes.

Transparent operations

Rules, dates, match format and official contact details are published before competition begins.

02 / Format

16 teams. Group stage into playoffs.

Group stage matches are best-of-three. The top teams advance to a single-elimination playoff bracket. Grand Final is best-of-five. Exact bracket seeding is published after registration closes.

Lobby settings

Game mode: Captains Mode · Cheats: disabled · Spectators: enabled · Server: Europe · Lobby hosted by tournament administration.

Series

Group stage and playoffs: BO3. Grand Final: BO5. Side/pick procedure is communicated to captains before each series.

04 / Rules

Core tournament rules

Full rules may be expanded before registration closes; material competitive changes will not be introduced after the event begins.

Eligibility

Players must use the Steam/Dota account registered with their team. Account substitution requires administrator approval.

Conduct

Cheating, match fixing, account sharing and intentional abuse of game-breaking exploits are prohibited.

Lobby & delays

Teams must join the assigned lobby on time. Technical issues must be reported to administration immediately.

Administration

Administrators resolve disputes using available lobby, match and communication records. Decisions apply consistently to all participants.

Community streaming
Community streams may use DotaTV and their own original production. No exclusive broadcast audio or camera feed is granted by this page.
