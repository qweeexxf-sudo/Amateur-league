01 / About

Independent amateur competition.

Riftline Open 2026 is an independently organized online Dota 2 event for amateur European teams. This page is the public source for the event date, competition format, rules and organizer contact.

Open entry

Team applications

Teams apply with a captain contact and Steam profiles for their starting roster. Participation is confirmed by tournament administration.

Online

European servers

Matches are played online using European Dota 2 servers. Administrators coordinate assigned lobbies and match-day issues.

Independent

Clear affiliation

Riftline Open is a community event and does not claim sponsorship, endorsement or professional circuit affiliation with Valve.

02 / Competition

16-team single elimination.

The event is designed for a single competition day. Early rounds use shorter series so the bracket can progress reliably; deciding rounds use multi-game series.

Bracket

Series format

Round of 16: BO1 · Quarterfinals: BO1 · Semifinals: BO3 · Grand Final: BO3. Pairings are communicated to confirmed captains after registration closes.

Lobby standard

Competitive settings

Captains Mode · cheats disabled · spectators enabled · European server region · organizer-coordinated lobby.

Roster

Five starting players

Players compete using the Steam/Dota accounts submitted with their team. Any roster substitution requires administrator approval.

Fees & prizes

Free participation

There is no entry fee. No prize pool is currently announced. Any prize information will be published before competition begins.

04 / Rules

Core tournament rules.

The same competitive and conduct standards apply to every participating team. Administrative decisions are based on available match, lobby and communication records.

Eligibility & accounts

Players must use their registered Steam/Dota account. Account sharing or playing for another registered player is prohibited.

Competitive integrity

Cheating, match fixing, external game manipulation and intentional abuse of game-breaking exploits are prohibited.

Lobby & readiness

Teams must join their assigned lobby and be ready when instructed. Technical problems must be reported to administration as soon as they occur.

Disconnects & disputes

Pauses and technical incidents are handled by the tournament administrator according to the circumstances and available game information. Teams must follow administrator instructions before resuming play.

Conduct

Threats, targeted harassment and deliberate disruption of tournament operations may result in warnings, game loss or removal from the event.

Administration

Tournament administration may resolve unforeseen situations in a way intended to preserve competitive fairness. Material rule changes will not be introduced after competition begins.
